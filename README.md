# Chicken Road New Game: Chicken Road 2.0 - What You Need to Know

InOut Games has followed up its hit crash title with **Chicken Road 2.0**, a sequel that keeps the step-by-step cashout loop but changes the setting, the risk ladder and the math. If you already play the original Chicken Road, here is what is new and what stays the same.

## What's new in Chicken Road 2.0

- **New setting.** The chicken now crosses a busy highway instead of a row of manhole covers. The cartoon style is the same, with reworked animations.
- **Longer roads.** The four difficulty levels now have 30, 25, 22 and 18 lanes (Easy to Hardcore). The original used shorter paths.
- **Lower RTP.** Chicken Road 2.0 runs at **95.5%**, compared with **98%** in the original.
- **Same win cap.** The maximum win stays at around **$20,000** per round, with casino limits on top.
- **Bets** run from about $0.01 to $200, depending on the operator.

## What stays the same

You still pick a stake and a difficulty, move the chicken one step at a time and decide after every step whether to cash out. One wrong step still ends the round. Rounds remain provably fair where the casino enables it.

## Should you switch?

It depends on how you play:

| You prefer | Better choice |
| --- | --- |
| Highest long-term return | Original Chicken Road (98% RTP) |
| A fresh look and longer runs | Chicken Road 2.0 |
| Short, high-risk sessions | Either, on Hardcore |

The 2.5-point RTP gap is small per round but adds up over thousands of rounds. For regular players, the original is still the stronger pick on numbers alone.
